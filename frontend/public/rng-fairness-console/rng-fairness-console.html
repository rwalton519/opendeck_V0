Watch a shuffle prove itself

Every click below runs the real commit → cut → reveal → verify sequence from commitRevealEngine.js, using SHA-256 and HMAC-SHA256 through your browser's own Web Crypto API. Nothing is server-side and nothing is simulated — the hashes on screen are the actual output of the math.
