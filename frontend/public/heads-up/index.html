Fairness Proof

This table is synced across tabs and devices: seats, the deck commit/reveal, and hand results are computed once by a small dedicated backend endpoint (/heads-up/*, see routers/headsUpDemoRouter.js) and pushed to every open tab live over Server-Sent Events — open a second tab and you'll see the same table update in both places. Live table state (seats, current hand) has no database and resets whenever the backend restarts, but the Hand History panel is different: every finished hand is written to a permanent demo_hand_history database record (shared with the Cash Game Demo table) that survives restarts, so it's a real historical ledger rather than a per-session log. The deck order (2→A per suit, ♠♥♦♣), the HMAC-SHA256-seeded Fisher-Yates shuffle, and the SHA-256 commit/reveal are the same algorithm the production fairness panel uses, which is also what lets hole cards actually stay private between the two seats until showdown. Betting runs a normal four-street structure (preflop, flop, turn, river) with fold/check/call/bet/raise available on your turn; a hand that doesn't get through to showdown ends the moment one player folds. Heads-up blinds follow the usual rule: the dealer posts the small blind and acts first preflop, then acts last on every later street. There are no house bots at this table — the hand starts automatically once a second real visitor takes the open seat. A player who busts can rebuy for $500 from their own seat; there's currently no way to stand up and free a seat for someone else once both are taken.
