Fairness Proof

This table isn't a poker game - there's nobody to seat, nothing to bet, and nothing hidden. Six fixed seats give each shuffle's randomness somewhere recognizable to land: click Deal and the server commits a fresh seed, shuffles a full 52-card deck with the exact same HMAC-SHA256-seeded Fisher-Yates engine as Cash Game Demo and Heads Up, deals two hole cards to each seat plus a full five-card board, and reveals the seed immediately alongside the result - there's no reason to delay the reveal here the way a betting table would, since nobody is acting on hidden information. Every seat's best five-card hand is scored and the winner(s) highlighted, then the whole thing - hand number, cards, winner, and the seed/commit-hash pair anyone can independently recompute and verify - is written to the same permanent demo_hand_history ledger shared with the other two demo tables (distinguished by room_key), so hand numbers are real, durable identifiers you can use to point someone back at a specific past hand. This table is synced live across every open tab over the same Server-Sent Events mechanism as the other demo tables (/RNG-shuffle-tester/*, see routers/rngShuffleTesterRouter.js) - open a second tab and you'll see the same hand land in both at once.
