Fairness Proof

This table is synced across tabs and devices: all 7 seats, the deck commit/reveal, and hand results are computed once by a small dedicated backend endpoint (/cashgame-demo/*, see routers/cashGameDemoRouter.js) and pushed to every open tab live over Server-Sent Events — open a second tab or sign in as Ach elsewhere and you'll see the same table update in both places. Live table state (seats, current hand) has no database and resets whenever the backend restarts, but the Hand History panel is different: every finished hand is written to a permanent demo_hand_history database record (shared with the Heads Up demo table) that survives restarts, so it's a real historical ledger rather than a per-session log. The deck order (2→A per suit, ♠♥♦♣), the HMAC-SHA256-seeded Fisher-Yates shuffle, and the SHA-256 commit/reveal are the same algorithm the production fairness panel uses — just running server-side now instead of duplicated per-tab, which is also what lets hole cards actually stay private between seats until showdown. Betting runs a normal four-street structure (preflop, flop, turn, river) with fold/check/call/bet/raise available on your turn; hands that don't get through to showdown end the moment everyone but one player has folded. Blinds are posted and rotated automatically. Three house seats play automatically so the table isn't empty; they rebuy on their own when busted.
